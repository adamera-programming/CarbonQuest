# CarbonQuest
CarbonQuest is a national award-winning mobile app designed to help individuals understand and reduce their environmental impact. The app allows users to track their daily activities, estimate their carbon footprint, and receive personalized recommendations to live more sustainably.


**Motivation:**
Many climate change education tools provide general information but lack personalized, actionable feedback. CarbonQuest addresses this gap by helping users understand their individual environmental impact and providing targeted suggestions to reduce their carbon emissions.


**Features:**
- Carbon Footprint Estimation – Calculates estimated emissions based on users' daily activities.
- Interactive Chatbot – Provides personalized sustainability advice and answers questions about climate impact.
- Educational Insights – Teaches users about emissions, climate change, and practical ways to reduce their footprint.


**Recognition:**
The CarbonQuest mobile application was presented to Congressman Herbert Conaway and his congressional staff, where the app and its potential for promoting climate awareness and sustainable behavior were demonstrated.


**What I Learned:**
- Developing a mobile application from concept to implementation
- Translating real-world environmental challenges into software-based solutions
- Designing user-focused interfaces that encourage sustainable behavior
